## About

I work in enterprise artificial intelligence strategy at a major Canadian bank, where I lead programs that bring generative AI into everyday business use with the governance and risk controls a regulated institution needs. My teaching draws on that practice. I help business students and working professionals understand what AI can and cannot do, how to judge a use case, and how to adopt these tools responsibly. I hold an MBA from the University of North Carolina and a master of engineering degree, and I began my career as a software engineer, so I can move comfortably between the boardroom view of AI and the technical view underneath it.
